# Keyword map

This map connects search intent to TMSAMPARK URLs. It is a planning document, not a traffic forecast.

## What was actually checked

On 17 October 2026 a public web search was used to see whether reliable India search volumes were available without a keyword tool login.

- Third-party pages discuss Indian keyword research in general and recommend Google Keyword Planner, Search Console, and a manual SERP review. They do not publish an auditable volume and difficulty pair for this site’s service list.
- One third-party snapshot page for the query “setting up a company in india” displayed about 260 searches and an average CPC of $1.48. That figure is not from Google Keyword Planner, the page does not document the month or location filter clearly, and it should not be used as a target.
- Broad claims such as “10,000+ searches” on marketing blogs were ignored.

No keyword difficulty scores are stated here, because none were measured in Ahrefs, Semrush, or Keyword Planner for this project.

## How to replace this with real numbers

1. Open Google Keyword Planner, location India, language English.
2. Paste the queries below. Record average monthly searches and competition as Planner shows them.
3. Repeat for Hindi queries if you publish Hindi pages.
4. In Ahrefs or Semrush, record keyword difficulty for the same list, India database.
5. After launch, use Search Console’s queries report. Those impressions are the only volumes that describe this domain.
6. Review the sheet quarterly. Do not paste tool screenshots into the website.

Treat head terms such as “GST registration” and “company registration” as competitive until Planner and a SERP review say otherwise. Prefer a specific service page over a city doorway page.
